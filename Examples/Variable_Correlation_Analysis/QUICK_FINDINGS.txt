Body Size ↔ Resting Heart Rate (NEGATIVE CORRELATION)

- Athletes and tall individuals typically have lower resting HR

#5 🍔 DIET-CARDIOVASCULAR CONNECTION
================================================================================
Dietary Energy Intake ↔ Resting Heart Rate (NEGATIVE)

Calories → Heart Rate: r = -0.41 (n=1,685)

Type: Dietary ↔ Cardiovascular (Cross-domain)

INTERPRETATION:
- Higher calorie consumption → LOWER heart rate
- Likely confounded by body size (larger people eat more, have lower HR)
- Or athletic individuals (higher intake, lower HR due to fitness)

ACTION: Control for body size when studying diet-cardiovascular relationships
