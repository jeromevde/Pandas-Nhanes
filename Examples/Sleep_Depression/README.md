# Sleep Hours vs Depression Score

## Research Question
What is the relationship between sleep duration and depression screening scores in US adults?

## Data Source
NHANES 2015-2016 cycle
- **SleepHours** (SLD010H): Self-reported hours of sleep per night
- **DepressionScore** (DPQ010): PHQ-9 depression screening score (0-27)
- **Gender** (RIAGENDR): Male/Female

## Key Findings
- U-shaped relationship: Optimal sleep ~7-8 hours
- Both short (<6h) and long (>9h) sleep associated with higher depression scores
- PHQ-9 score peaks at extreme sleep durations

## Hypothesis
Sleep deprivation and oversleeping are both associated with increased depressive symptoms, consistent with sleep hygiene recommendations.

## Public Health Implications
Screening for sleep disorders could be valuable in depression prevention and treatment.
